| Trends and Nature of Petty Offenses | How many petty cases have your offices (Judiciary) or Police station (For Police only) received, January to December 2025 (last year)? (Is this an exact number or estimate? Probe for records if estimates)
Would you say this number of these petty cases you received is: 
Very high, b. High, c. Moderate, d. Low, e. Very low 
If High:
Why do you think the number of petty cases that came to your institution is high?
What can be done to reduce the number of petty cases coming to your office/police station?
If low:
In your opinion, what were the main factors leading to low levels of petty cases coming to your institutions (the formal justice system)?
What measures do you think could reduce the influx of petty cases into the formal justice system last year? |
| Diversion and Referral Practices | Of the petty cases you received above, how many did you handle?
And how many cases did you refer to the Local Councils (LCs)? (Probe: Are there other community mechanisms where you referred cases to)
What factors influence your decision to;
Divert petty cases to LCs
Not to divert petty cases to LCs 
What challenges or barriers hinder diversion of cases to Local Councils? (Probe for legal, procedural, institutional, or community-related factors that affect diversion practices)
What can be done to improve or strengthen diversion of petty cases to LCs? 
Have you encountered situations where cases were inappropriately handled at police or court instead of being referred to the LC? (If yes, explain why). 
How many Local Council Courts have supervised in the last 12 months?
Would you say this number is high or low?
If High: 
What are some of the factors that enabled this high supervision
How helpful was your supervision to these Local Court courts?
If Low:
In your opinion, why do you think this supervision was low
Other than financial and logistical support, magistrates improve supervision of local courts? |
| Capacity and Functionality of Local Councils | In your opinion, do Local Councils have the capacity to handle disputes effectively? 
If yes.  What strengths do Local Councils currently possess in handling disputes?
If no, why do you think LCs do not have the capacity to handle disputes effectively? 
What can be done to improve  LCs’ capacity to handle disputes effectively?
What are the major weaknesses or gaps affecting Local Councils in handling disputes? Probe: How effective are Local Councils in record-keeping and documentation of cases? 
Do Local Councils adequately distinguish between cases that should remain at community level and those requiring formal justice intervention? Please explain. 
What support or capacity-building do Local Councils require to improve their effectiveness?
In your view, how well do Local Councils coordinate with police and courts in handling disputes?
If well. What is the nature of this collaboration?
If not well. Why is coordination with Local Councils in handling disputes low? |
| Community Trust and Legitimacy of LCs | In your opinion do you think communities trust LC to handle disputes and offenses?
If yes: 
 Why do you think communities trust LC to handle disputes and offenses?
What indicators or signs demonstrate that communities trust Local Councils? 
If not
Why do you think communities don’t trust LC to handle disputes and offenses?
What indicators or signs demonstrate that communities do not trust Local Councils? 
What could be done to improve community trust in LCs?
Are there particular groups in the community that are less likely to trust Local Councils? If Yes, Which groups and why? |
| Recommendations and Closing | What reforms or improvements would strengthen collaboration between Local Councils and the formal justice system? 
What recommendations would you make to improve community-level dispute resolution and diversion practices in Uganda? 
Is there anything else you would like to add regarding the handling of petty offenders and the role of Local Councils? |
