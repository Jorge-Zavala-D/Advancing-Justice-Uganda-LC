- Purpose of Engagement
This Consultancy Agreement sets out the terms under which the Evaluation Lead will support the Client in the design, technical oversight, analysis, and reporting of the baseline evaluation and related impact-evaluation architecture for the Access to Justice Programme in Uganda, implemented by Communication for Development Foundation Uganda (CDFU) with support from the Legatum Foundation, in the districts of Bushenyi, Rubirizi, and Sheema.
The purpose of this engagement is to generate high-quality, reliable, policy-relevant, and academically rigorous evidence to inform programme design, implementation, monitoring, future impact assessment, and related scholarly outputs. The work includes the baseline design and reporting package, technical support to implementation, learning and monitoring inputs, and the explicit preparation of the research architecture for the randomized controlled trial (RCT), subsequent endline analysis, and related academic outputs.
The Parties acknowledge that the current fixed-fee arrangement under this Agreement primarily covers the baseline and inception scope described below. The Parties further acknowledge that additional technical work related to the RCT, endline analysis, econometric analysis, ethics/IRB review, academic paper writing, and journal submission may be required and shall be governed by the additional-services clause in Section 7 below.
- Role of the Evaluation Lead
The Evaluation Lead shall serve as the principal technical lead for the evaluation and academic/research components of the assignment. This role includes leadership on research design, econometric and statistical planning, sampling and randomization design, data quality standards, analysis, interpretation, reporting, and the academic component of the project.
For avoidance of doubt, the Evaluation Lead’s role is technical and research-oriented and does not include day-to-day project operations, field logistics, enumerator supervision, transport arrangements, local permissions, or routine implementation management, except in an advisory capacity where required.
The Evaluation Lead shall work remotely, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing. Any travel by the Evaluation Lead for fieldwork, training, dissemination, or related activities shall require separate written agreement and must be accompanied by full coverage of travel, accommodation, per diem, and security-related costs.
- Scope of Services
- Baseline / Inception Scope Covered Under the Current Agreement
Under the current fixed-fee arrangement, the Evaluation Lead shall provide the following services:
Evaluation design and inception support, including:
review and refinement of the evaluation framework;
finalization of the intervention and evaluation design;
development of the sampling and randomization framework;
preparation of the inception report and related design materials.
Technical support to baseline implementation, including:
finalization of data collection tools from the technical perspective;
review of pilot/pre-test outputs;
technical guidance on data quality assurance procedures;
technical support to implementation decisions relevant to the validity of the study.
Data management and baseline analysis, including:
review, cleaning, and technical validation of raw data;
preparation of the baseline administrative dataset and/or analysis dataset;
preparation of codebooks and data quality memos;
analysis and interpretation of baseline findings.
Baseline reporting and dissemination support, including:
Develop the baseline report;
final review and approval of core technical deliverables;
participation in one virtual presentation of findings, if requested.
Initial learning and monitoring support, including:
technical input into monitoring and learning planning;
technical input into action planning for intervention implementation;
Generate learning briefs within the limits of the current fixed-fee scope.

- Additional / Extended Services Outside the Current Fixed Fee
The following services are explicitly contemplated by the Parties but are not fully covered by the current fixed-fee arrangement unless otherwise agreed in writing:
technical design and support for the full RCT implementation;
ethics / IRB preparation, revision, and submission support;
advanced monitoring of the randomized design and treatment fidelity beyond the initial baseline scope;
endline design, endline dataset construction, and endline analysis;
advanced econometric analysis, causal inference estimation, and robustness analysis;
cost-effectiveness analysis beyond the baseline scope;
drafting of academic outputs, including working papers, conference papers, journal manuscripts, response memos to reviewers, and journal submission support;
any technical work extending materially beyond the estimated 16 days covered by the current fixed-fee arrangement.
Such additional services shall be activated only upon written agreement between the Parties and shall be billed at the same daily rate specified in Section 7, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

- Responsibilities of the Parties
The Evaluation Lead shall be responsible for:
- the technical design and methodological leadership of the evaluation;
- final approval and/or direct preparation of the technical deliverables assigned to him under this Agreement;
- technical review and final approval of data collection tools from the research perspective;
- technical review of the pre-test and data collection outputs;
- technical review, cleaning, and analysis of the relevant datasets;
- Generate the baseline report and related analytical outputs;
- technical input into learning, monitoring, and research planning.
The Client, together with the designated coordinator, CDFU, and relevant implementation partners, shall be responsible for:
- obtaining and maintaining access to police records, court records, Local Council Court registers, and partner monitoring data;
- fieldwork operations, including staffing, local permissions, transport, logistics, enumerator hiring, and day-to-day supervision;
- data security and storage during fieldwork and initial collection stages;
- coordination with CDFU and Legatum on implementation decisions;
- timely provision to the Evaluation Lead of all data, documents, tools, comments, and information required for the Evaluation Lead to perform the agreed services.
The Parties expressly acknowledge that the quality and timing of the Evaluation Lead’s work are dependent upon the Client’s timely provision of the required data, documentation, and feedback.

- Fees, Days of Work, and Payment Terms
For the baseline/inception scope covered by the current Agreement, the Client shall pay the Evaluation Lead a total professional fee of USD 4,800, corresponding to an estimated 16 working days at a rate of USD 300 per day. The Parties acknowledge that this 16-day estimate reflects the currently defined baseline-related scope and associated initial technical support only.

- Indicative Day Allocation Within the Current Fixed Fee

This allocation is indicative and may be adjusted across tasks, provided the total effort remains within the 16-day equivalent covered by the current fixed fee.
- Payment Schedule and Additional Days
The total professional fee of USD 4,800 shall be paid as follows:
- 50% (USD 2,400) upon signing of this Agreement;
- 30% (USD 1,440) upon submission and written acceptance of the final baseline report and associated baseline deliverables;
- 20% (USD 960) upon submission and written acceptance of the agreed learning/monitoring deliverables covered under the current scope.
If the project ends early after completion of the baseline-related deliverables, all unpaid baseline amounts due in relation to completed baseline deliverables shall become immediately payable.


The Parties acknowledge that the post-baseline RCT, monitoring, endline, advanced econometric analysis, ethics/IRB processes, and academic-writing components are likely to require additional effort beyond the 16 days covered by the current fixed-fee arrangement.
Accordingly:
- any work beyond the current baseline-related fixed-fee scope shall require written approval by the Parties;
- such work shall be compensated at USD 300 per additional working day, unless otherwise agreed in writing;
- the Parties anticipate that the extended RCT / endline / academic component may require at least an additional 10 working days, subject to final agreement once the RCT, endline data collection, and paper-writing plan are finalized.


- Confidentiality
The Evaluation Lead agrees to keep confidential any non-public information obtained during the course of this engagement and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Client, unless required by law.
Any additional confidentiality agreement, data-sharing agreement, or related document referred to in connection with this engagement must be shared with the Evaluation Lead before signature and shall bind the Evaluation Lead only after review and written agreement.
- Intellectual Property, Academic Use, and Attribution
All reports, datasets, analytical files, and work products specifically produced under this Agreement for the project shall, upon full payment of the relevant fees, be available for use by the Parties to this Agreement for programme, reporting, and research purposes.
Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Evaluation Lead from retaining and reusing his general methods, templates, code structure, analytical frameworks, know-how, or non-project-specific tools in future work.
The Parties acknowledge that a central purpose of the Evaluation Lead’s involvement is the development of the academic and research component of the project. Accordingly:
any data-sharing arrangements for academic use shall be discussed and agreed between the Parties in writing;
any future academic publication, working paper, journal article, conference paper, or related scholarly output arising from the project shall be subject to prior written agreement on authorship, acknowledgment, data access, and publication procedures;
the Evaluation Lead shall have the right to list the project on his CV, professional biography, and portfolio once the project is public or with the Client’s prior approval.
For clarity, ownership shall not transfer automatically to any third party not party to this Agreement unless separately agreed in writing by the Parties.

- Independent Contractor Status
The Evaluation Lead is engaged as an independent contractor and shall be solely responsible for all taxes, insurance, bank charges, and any other statutory obligations arising from this engagement. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating an employer–employee relationship between the Evaluation Lead and the Client. For the avoidance of doubt, the Evaluation Lead’s contractual relationship is exclusively with the Client, and no employment or contractual relationship is established with the Legatum Foundation.
- Term and Termination
This Agreement shall commence upon signature by both Parties and shall remain in effect until completion of the agreed services and submission of the relevant final deliverables, unless terminated earlier in writing by either Party. In the event of termination, the Client shall pay the Evaluation Lead for all work completed and all deliverables submitted up to the date of termination, including any amounts due for completed baseline work and any separately approved additional work.
- Governing Law
This agreement shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of Uganda.
